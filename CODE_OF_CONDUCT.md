# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SkillSphere is a collaborative software project. We want project spaces to remain welcoming to contributors with different backgrounds, roles, and levels of experience. Discussions should be direct and technically rigorous without becoming hostile or personal.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:
- Communicate respectfully and assume good faith where reasonable.
- Keep criticism focused on code, decisions, evidence, and project outcomes.
- Explain technical feedback clearly and constructively.
- Welcome questions from new contributors.
- Respect different opinions and experience levels.
- Give credit for work and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing personal information.
- Follow GitHub's terms and applicable laws.
- Keep project discussions relevant and avoid spam.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable conduct includes:
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
- Personal insults, humiliation, or repeated hostile comments.
- Unwanted sexual attention or sexualized harassment.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith participation.
- Impersonation, malicious links, or spam.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and project-related communication where participants represent or engage with SkillSphere.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not publish sensitive details in a public issue. Include relevant links, dates, and a factual description when it is safe to do so.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly as practical. The maintainer may request clarification or evidence and will handle reports with discretion.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity, context, and repetition, the maintainer may:
1. Provide a reminder of the expected standard.
2. Issue a warning and request that behavior stop.
3. Remove inappropriate content where platform controls permit.
4. Temporarily restrict participation.
5. Permanently restrict participation in project spaces.

Severe behavior may result in immediate restriction. The maintainer will consider impact and context rather than applying a mechanical rule to every situation.

## Confidentiality and retaliation

Information about a report will be shared only as reasonably necessary to review and address it. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter or anyone assisting with a review is not acceptable.

## Technical disagreement

Disagreement is welcome. Contributors may challenge a design, request evidence, reject a proposal, or identify serious defects. They must do so without attacking people.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in this repository, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a constructive project environment.
